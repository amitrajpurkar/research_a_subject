# The Rajpurkar Family Chat — A Storyline, Dec 2022 – Oct 2026

*Sources: (1) WhatsApp group "rjp_fmly_archive" (iPhone-style export with media, ending 27 Sep 2026) and (2) a second, text-only Android export from the "Amit 305" number (`rjp_from305.txt`, merged 6 Oct 2026), which adds the Dec 2022 – Jan 2024 prologue, a missing week in July 2025 and the days to 5 Oct 2026. Quarters below are in chronological order. Names are consolidated as: **Amit** (Amit Rajpurkar + "Amit 305", shown as "Amit on Motorola" in the second export), **Aaji** / "Kandivli Aaji" (Mummy Viva + Prita Rajpurkar), **Aajoba** (Pappaji Viva, Nandkumar Rajpurkar), **Yuvan**. Other recurring people: **Dhanesh** (Amit's elder son, not a sender) and **Prajakta** (Amit's wife).*

## The shape of the story

- **Who and where.** Aajoba and Aaji live in Kandivli, Mumbai; Amit, Prajakta, Dhanesh and Yuvan live in Jacksonville, Florida. The chat is the bridge across a 9½-hour time difference: Amit's 4 am-Florida / 1:30 pm-Mumbai call ritual, and the photos, running logs, forwards and "I'm sleeping today" notes that surround it.
- **Volume.** ~15,000 messages between 6 Jan 2024 and 27 Sep 2026 in the first archive: Amit ≈ 6,600, Aaji ≈ 4,300, Aajoba ≈ 4,050, Yuvan ≈ 20 (he is added to the group late), plus 4 replies from Meta AI. The second export adds ≈ 4,700 entries the first archive lacks — about 4,470 from 27 Dec 2022 to 6 Jan 2024 (mostly photo dumps and short check-ins from Amit and Aaji), 140 from 16–24 Jul 2025, and 98 from 28 Sep to 5 Oct 2026 — so the story now runs from **27 Dec 2022 to 5 Oct 2026**. A blank window runs 19 Dec 2024 – 19 Jan 2025 (disappearing messages were on); image and audio files exist only from 19 Jan 2025 onward.
- **Recurring threads.** (1) Amit's running, sleep and now triathlon; (2) the boys' achievements — robotics, SAT/ACT, hackathons, history fairs, art contests, Dhanesh's move to university; (3) Aajoba and Aaji's trips across Maharashtra and Gujarat, nearly always documented with photo dumps; (4) astronomy (NEFAS, telescopes, eclipses); (5) the arrival of AI in the family — from "@Meta AI" to Aaji setting an aarti to music with AI; (6) festivals kept in two countries; (7) Aaji's daily torrent of devotional, political and moral forwards; (8) an easy, affectionate, slightly teasing family tone.
- **Turning points.** The grandparents' two months in Jacksonville (24 May – 24 Jul 2023); the Meta AI experiments and the Washington D.C. road trip (Dec 2023); photos begin flowing (Jan 2025); Dhanesh's Houston robotics trip (Apr 2025); an emotional exchange between Amit and Aaji (24–25 Oct 2025); Aaji's eye operations (Jan 2026); both boys win in the EMERGE hackathon (Feb 2026); Dhanesh's UCF acceptance (Jan 2026), graduation and move to his dorm (Jun–Aug 2026); the 17-day New York road trip (May–Jun 2026); Yuvan joins the group, is removed twice, and is restored (Jun–Aug 2026).

## 2024 · Q1 (Jan–Mar) — "Art with STEM", Sydney, and a Florida spring

**Cast on stage:** Amit and his family (wife Prajakta, sons Dhanesh and Yuvan — "Yuvi"/"Motu") in Jacksonville, Florida; Aajoba and Kandivli Aaji in Mumbai (Kandivli), with Aajoba as the chat's steady, encouraging conversationalist and Aaji as its relentless forwarder of Marathi/Hindi devotional, patriotic and humorous clips.

**Opening scene (6 Jan 2024).** The archive opens with Amit sharing the FIRST Robotics Competition manual Q&A link and explaining that Dhanesh's robotics team has just received the season's challenge — "art with STEM", i.e. STEAM — with a six-hour strategy session set for the next day. Image text came from automated English OCR, so Marathi/Hindi images are not transcribed and some inferences from OCR (for example certificates, scores and contest names) should be checked against the originals. YouTube/Facebook link contents could not be retrieved. Some interpretive remarks (for example the 24–25 Oct 2025 exchange) are readings of context, not stated facts. Sensitive identifiers appearing in screenshots (candidate IDs, plate/VIN numbers) were deliberately left out.
